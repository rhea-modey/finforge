# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Keep Tidewater Outfitters useful, approachable, and grounded in real trips. The brand refresh should sharpen the visual system and product language without making the company feel overly polished or corporate.

## Priorities

- Refresh core campaign templates, photography guidance, and storefront copy.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, built around practical topics such as trip preparation, gear care, and choosing equipment for changing weather.
- Pilot a co-marketing program with a small group of complementary outdoor partners.
- Build seasonal content around checklists, field notes, staff picks, and customer questions.
- Improve post-purchase messaging with care tips and relevant product suggestions.

## Channel approach

Email will carry launches, field guides, and webinar follow-up. Social content should favor demonstrations, packing advice, and behind-the-scenes product testing. Paid media will support selected campaigns after creative and landing pages are validated. Partner activity should be limited to brands whose audience and service standards fit ours.

## Measurement

Review engagement, repeat visits, webinar participation, partner-sourced traffic, and conversion quality by channel. Capture lessons after each campaign and use them to shape the next quarter.

Budget remains pending Q2 close and channel review.
