# Notes from your predecessor

P. Guerrero — a few things that will save you some hunting at close:

- The bank’s own service fees and charges usually appear near the end of the statement. We have historically picked them up and booked them during the close.
- Do not assume the June vendor-bill coding is clean. A couple of bills may be sitting in the wrong expense account; when the GL and support disagree, the documents in `bills/` are authoritative.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. The one-off items that arrived in June were never included in the plan, so they are the main explanation for the large variances.
- Payroll timing is different this time: the second June run pays in early July. Check the payroll register and handle the cutoff accordingly.
