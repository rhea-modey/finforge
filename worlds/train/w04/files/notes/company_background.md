# Company background

Harborview Consulting LLP began in 2018 when two former in-house operators realized that many growing organizations needed experienced help without another permanent layer of management. They set up a compact advisory practice built around plain language, practical recommendations, and a willingness to stay through implementation rather than leave clients with a presentation deck.

The firm sells project-based strategy, operations, process improvement, and management advisory services. Engagements often start with interviews and workflow reviews, then move into facilitated planning, decision support, and hands-on coordination with client teams. Harborview’s clients span established local businesses, health organizations, consumer brands, and industrial companies.

The team has a distinctly working-session culture. Partners lead engagements directly, consultants trade templates freely, and the office is known for marked-up whiteboards, strong coffee, and concise internal notes. Specialists in legal, accounting, marketing, and technology are brought in when a project calls for expertise outside the core team.

Currently, Harborview Consulting LLP is balancing active client delivery with the routine work of sharpening its market position and improving internal systems. Leadership is refining service packages, documenting repeatable methods, and developing a steadier referral pipeline while preserving the responsive, senior-led approach that clients value.
