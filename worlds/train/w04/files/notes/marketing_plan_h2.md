# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Harborview Consulting LLP as the practical advisory firm for teams that need senior attention and usable answers, not extra ceremony. Keep the tone confident, specific, and human.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand story around hands-on strategy, operations, and implementation.
- Publish short field notes drawn from recurring client challenges, with identifying details removed.
- Host two focused webinars per quarter, each built around a real operating question rather than a broad trend.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with complementary legal, accounting, and technology firms.
- Give partners a simple follow-up kit for referrals, event attendees, and dormant relationships.

## Campaign themes

For Q3, emphasize clearing operational bottlenecks and turning plans into accountable work. For Q4, shift toward planning discipline, management cadence, and readiness for the next operating cycle.

## Channels

Use the firm website as the source of record, then adapt material for email, professional social channels, partner newsletters, and webinar follow-up. Favor useful checklists, brief case examples, and direct partner commentary over generic thought leadership.

## Measurement

Track qualified conversations, referral activity, webinar engagement, content reuse, and movement from first contact to scoped discussion. Review results at each quarter close and retain only the tactics that produce credible client interest.
