# Company background

Halcyon Grid Software began in 2019 when a utility-operations analyst and a software engineer compared notes after yet another planning meeting run through spreadsheets, email threads, and marked-up screenshots. They believed smaller energy teams deserved the same operational visibility as large enterprises without having to deploy a sprawling consulting project.

The company now sells a hosted workflow platform for teams that coordinate grid maintenance, field requests, capacity planning, and internal approvals. Customers use configurable work queues, audit trails, dashboards, and integrations to keep operational information in one place. The product is designed to sit alongside existing utility and engineering systems rather than replace them.

The team is compact and practical. Engineers regularly join customer calls, the support lead maintains an unusually detailed library of implementation notes, and sales conversations tend to include live product walk-throughs rather than polished theater. Remote work is common, though the group still gathers for planning sessions and product reviews.

Halcyon Grid Software is currently focused on dependable service, careful product improvements, and stronger onboarding for growing customer teams. Leadership is also refining partner marketing and internal operating routines so the business can scale without losing the direct, helpful style that shaped its early customer relationships.
