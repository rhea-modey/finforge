# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Halcyon Grid Software as the practical operations layer for grid teams that have outgrown spreadsheets but do not want a heavy transformation program. Keep the voice informed, direct, and grounded in day-to-day customer work.

## Q3 priorities

- Refresh core messaging around workflow visibility, auditability, and faster handoffs.
- Update the website's product pages with clearer use cases and shorter demonstrations.
- Run two educational webinars featuring product specialists and customer-facing staff.
- Build a reusable field guide from common implementation questions.
- Recruit a small group of integration and advisory partners for a co-marketing pilot.

## Q4 priorities

- Publish customer stories focused on operational outcomes and adoption lessons.
- Run two additional webinars, using Q3 attendance questions to shape the topics.
- Test partner-led roundtables for utility operations and engineering audiences.
- Equip sales with refreshed overview slides, demo narratives, and follow-up material.
- Review channel quality and document recommendations for the next planning cycle.

## Measures

Track qualified conversations, webinar engagement, content-assisted opportunities, partner participation, and movement from first meeting to product evaluation. Favor signals of genuine buying interest over raw traffic.

## Open items

Confirm campaign ownership, finalize the editorial calendar, select pilot partners, and align available spend after the Q2 close.
