# A few handoff notes

R. Halloway — a few things I learned the hard way:

- The bank's own service fees and charges usually appear near the end of the statement. They have historically been booked during close, so do not assume the ledger already has them.
- I was moving quickly in June, and the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and reclass the GL where needed.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. The one-off items that arrived in June were never part of the plan, and they are the reason the larger variances show up.
- Payroll timing is different this time: the second June run pays in early July. Check the payroll register and handle the accrual from there.
