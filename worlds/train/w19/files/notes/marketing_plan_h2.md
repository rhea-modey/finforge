# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction
Position Prairie State Goods Co. as the approachable wholesale partner for independent retailers that need useful merchandise, manageable ordering, and responsive service.

## Priorities
- Refresh the brand voice and core sales materials without losing the company’s practical, plainspoken character.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter for store owners and buyers, centered on assortment planning, seasonal displays, and smoother reordering.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary supplier and a small group of retail accounts.
- Build repeatable product stories that sales can reuse in email, line sheets, and customer conversations.
- Improve follow-up after samples, quotes, and webinar attendance.

## Quarter focus
### Q3
Finalize messaging, update the standard product presentation, prepare the webinar format, and recruit pilot partners. Establish a simple editorial rhythm around merchandising advice and new assortment highlights.

### Q4
Use lessons from Q3 to sharpen webinar topics, publish partner content, and equip sales with campaign follow-up notes. Feature practical seasonal ordering guidance rather than broad brand advertising.

## Measurement
Track qualified inquiries, webinar participation, partner engagement, sample requests, repeat ordering signals, and sales-team use of the refreshed materials. Review results at each quarter end before expanding the pilot.
