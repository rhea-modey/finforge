# Notes from the previous accountant

A. Whitcomb — a few things I learned the hard way:

- The bank slips its own fees and charges in near the end of the statement. We normally book those during close, not as they appear during the month, so do not stop the cash reconciliation too early.
- I was moving too fast in June, and the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and reclass the GL where needed.
- The budget was fixed at the start of Q2. None of the one-off June work made it into the plan, and those items should explain most of the large budget-to-actual variances.
- Payroll timing is unusual this month: the second June run pays in early July. Check the payroll register before recording the accrual.
