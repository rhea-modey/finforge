# Notes from your chatty predecessor

For G. Castellano — a few things I learned the hard way:

- Check the tail end of the bank statement carefully. The bank's own service fees and other charges usually appear near statement end, and we have historically booked them during close rather than earlier in the month.
- June got busy, so do not assume the vendor-bill expense coding is right. Compare the entries to the source documents in `bills/`; those documents are authoritative, and anything inconsistent should be corrected.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. The one-off items that arrived in June were never included in the plan, so they are the reason several of the large variances show up.
- Payroll timing matters this month: the back half of June pays in early July. The payroll register contains the relevant dates, so use it when preparing the accrual.
