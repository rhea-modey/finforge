# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Keep Juniper Goods Co. recognizable while sharpening the brand voice: useful, warm, and pleasantly unfussy. The refresh should improve consistency across product pages, email, paid media, packaging inserts, and partner materials without making the company feel overly polished.

## Priorities

- Refresh core brand language and create a practical messaging guide for the team.
- Improve product storytelling around materials, use cases, care, and gifting.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, using topics that naturally connect to home organization, thoughtful gifting, and everyday workspaces.
- Pilot partner co-marketing with a complementary brand. Keep the first collaboration narrow enough to evaluate audience fit and operating effort.
- Build reusable creative from customer questions, product demonstrations, and behind-the-scenes fulfillment content.
- Coordinate email and paid-media themes so customers encounter one clear campaign story at a time.

## Measurement

Review engagement quality, repeat visits, email response, partner-referred traffic, and product-page behavior. Separate short campaign observations from broader brand conclusions.

## Open items

Confirm campaign ownership, webinar speakers, partner shortlist, creative capacity, and channel mix after the Q2 close. Document lessons at each quarter-end and carry only the strongest tests forward.
