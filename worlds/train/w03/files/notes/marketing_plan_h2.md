# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Granite Peak Distribution as the practical, responsive wholesale partner for organizations that need reliable supplies without big-company runaround. The tone should be capable, local, and plainspoken.

## Priorities

- Refresh core brand language across sales sheets, email templates, and the website.
- Publish customer-focused material around ordering, fulfillment, and inventory planning.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter, led jointly by sales and operations.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with complementary local service providers.
- Give account managers reusable follow-up notes for dormant and recently opened accounts.
- Collect customer stories that emphasize responsiveness, consolidated shipments, and product knowledge.

## Quarter focus

### Q3

Complete the messaging refresh, update the most-used sales materials, and launch the webinar series. Select pilot partners based on customer overlap and operational fit.

### Q4

Expand the strongest webinar topics into short guides and email content. Review partner-pilot engagement, refine account outreach, and prepare recommendations for the next planning cycle.

## Measurement

Track qualified inquiries, webinar attendance, partner-sourced conversations, repeat engagement, and sales-team use of refreshed materials. Final channel choices remain subject to the Q2 close and leadership review.
