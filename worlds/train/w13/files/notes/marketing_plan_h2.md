# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## What we are trying to accomplish

Give Fathom Analytics Co. a clearer, more consistent market presence without sanding off the practical voice customers already recognize. The brand refresh should emphasize useful answers, approachable reporting, and fast adoption by operating teams.

## Core programs

- Complete the brand refresh across the website, sales materials, product screenshots, and customer-facing templates.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter. Keep the sessions tactical: reporting hygiene, metric ownership, and better operating reviews.
- Develop a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary software or advisory firm. Start narrowly, agree on audience and ownership, then document what is repeatable.
- Turn strong customer questions into short articles, product demonstrations, and sales enablement notes.
- Build a lightweight customer-story pipeline with support and account conversations as the primary source.

## Working approach

Use Q2 close findings to finalize the available budget and sequence the work. Favor reusable content over isolated campaigns, and avoid launching more channels than the team can maintain well.

## Measures to watch

Track qualified interest, webinar engagement, partner-sourced conversations, content reuse by sales, and whether refreshed messaging improves the quality of discovery calls. Review results at each quarter-end and adjust the mix rather than adding activity for its own sake.
