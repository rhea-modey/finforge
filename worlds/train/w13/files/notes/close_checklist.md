4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after verifying the amount has not already been posted to the GL.
5. Compare the June vendor-bill documents with the GL coding. Verify that each expense is in the correct account and reclass anything miscoded.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after items 1-5 have been booked.
