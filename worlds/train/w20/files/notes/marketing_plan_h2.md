# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Lakefront Strategy Group as the practical strategy partner for teams that need clear decisions, useful research, and implementation-ready plans. Keep the voice confident, specific, and free of big-firm jargon.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand system and tighten the core service descriptions.
- Publish client-friendly point-of-view pieces built around recurring market and growth questions.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter, with follow-up material suitable for sales conversations.
- Pilot a co-marketing program with a complementary service partner.
- Improve referral outreach through short, personal updates rather than broad promotional mailings.

## Q3 focus

Complete the messaging refresh, update key website pages, and prepare the first webinar series. Build a reusable editorial calendar and identify partner candidates with overlapping audiences but noncompeting services.

## Q4 focus

Run the partner pilot, continue the webinar cadence, and turn the strongest sessions into articles and sales enablement material. Gather qualitative feedback from prospects, clients, and referral sources.

## Measures

Track qualified conversations, referral activity, webinar attendance, follow-up engagement, and whether new inquiries align with the firm’s preferred work. Review results at quarter-end and adjust the mix before expanding any pilot.
