# Notes from your predecessor

K. Sorensen — a few practical things I learned the hard way:

- Check the tail end of the bank statement carefully. The bank’s own service fees and charges usually appear near statement end, and we historically book them during close.
- Do not trust the first-pass account coding on every June vendor bill. Some expense coding may be off; the supporting documents in `bills/` are authoritative, so use those when deciding whether to reclass.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. One-off items that arrived in June were never included in the plan, and they are the reason several variances look unusually large.
- Payroll timing is not consistent enough to guess. The pay date for the second monthly run varies, so verify the actual timing in the payroll register rather than assuming it pays in-month or in July.
