# June 2026 close — controller checklist

Prepared by K. Sorensen. Complete sequentially; no shortcuts.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 that pays in July; some months, every run pays in-month. If such a period exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities.
3. Confirm June prepaid amortization against the prepaid schedule and supporting contracts. Check the GL first to prevent duplication. If the entry is missing, book all June prepaid amortization in ONE combined journal entry. Apply the convention that a full month is taken in the month an item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after first confirming that it has not already been posted to the GL.
5. Compare June vendor-bill documents with the GL coding. Verify that each bill is charged to the correct expense account and reclass anything miscoded.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after items 1-5 have been booked.
