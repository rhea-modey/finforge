# Company background

Northlight Systems Inc. began in 2018 when a product manager and a software engineer grew tired of watching small operating teams coordinate important work through scattered spreadsheets, inbox threads, and improvised checklists. They built a lightweight internal tool, shared it with a few former colleagues, and found that the same problem appeared across many service businesses.

Today, Northlight Systems Inc. sells a hosted workflow platform that helps customers organize recurring processes, assign ownership, collect approvals, and keep a clear record of completed work. The product is designed for teams that need structure without the overhead of a large enterprise system. Implementation is intentionally practical, with templates, guided setup, and responsive customer support.

The company remains small and hands-on. Engineers regularly join customer calls, the support team contributes product ideas, and sales works closely with marketing rather than operating as a separate silo. Internal communication tends to be direct, friendly, and rich in shorthand developed over years of working together.

The current focus is steady execution: improving onboarding, refining reporting, strengthening partner relationships, and making the platform easier to adopt across customer departments. Leadership is also tightening routine operating processes as the business matures, while preserving the approachable culture that shaped the original product.
