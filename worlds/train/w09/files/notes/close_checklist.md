# June 2026 close — controller checklist

Prepared by J. Prather. Complete in sequence; no shortcuts.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 that pays in July. If one exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities; some months all payroll runs pay in-month.
3. Confirm June prepaid amortization against the prepaid schedule and supporting contracts. Check the GL first to prevent duplication. If the amortization is missing, book ONE combined journal entry. Apply the convention that a full month is taken in the month each prepaid item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after verifying the GL to ensure it has not already been recorded.
5. Compare the June vendor-bill documents with the GL coding. Verify that each bill is charged to the correct expense account and reclass anything miscoded.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after all entries and corrections from steps 1-5 have been booked.
