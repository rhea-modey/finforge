# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Northlight Systems Inc. as the practical workflow platform for teams that have outgrown shared spreadsheets but do not want a heavy enterprise rollout. Keep the voice clear, useful, and grounded in day-to-day operating problems.

## Priorities

- Refresh core messaging and visual presentation across the website, sales materials, and product tours.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, built around real workflow challenges rather than broad product demonstrations.
- Pilot partner co-marketing with a small group of implementation advisers and complementary software providers.
- Develop customer stories that show faster handoffs, clearer ownership, and more consistent process completion.
- Create a reusable campaign kit for sales, including short guides, email copy, webinar follow-up, and role-specific landing pages.

## Audience

Concentrate on operations leaders, department managers, and growing service teams that need repeatable processes without extensive administration.

## Measurement

Track qualified engagement, webinar participation, partner-sourced conversations, content-assisted opportunities, and movement from initial interest to product evaluation. Review results with sales each quarter and adjust topics based on customer questions.

## Open items

Confirm campaign sequencing after the Q2 close, identify willing customer storytellers, and select the first partner pilot. Final channel mix remains subject to team capacity.
