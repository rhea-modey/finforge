# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Redstone Advisory Group as the practical advisory firm for teams that need decisions, alignment, and execution support without layers of ceremony. The voice should be direct, experienced, and useful.

## Priorities

- Refresh the core story and visual system so proposals, presentation templates, and the website feel consistent.
- Publish a short field-note series drawn from recurring client questions, with emphasis on operating clarity and implementation.
- Host two webinars per quarter. Keep the format conversational, include a client-relevant worksheet, and repurpose each session into follow-up content.
- Pilot co-marketing with a small group of complementary partners. Start with shared roundtables and practical guides rather than broad promotional campaigns.
- Build a light referral-nurture rhythm for former clients and professional contacts.

## Quarter-by-quarter emphasis

### Q3

Finalize messaging, update priority materials, select webinar topics, and recruit pilot partners. Establish a simple editorial calendar and assign owners.

### Q4

Run the partner pilot, continue the webinar cadence, publish field notes, and collect usable audience feedback. Tighten calls to action based on what generates substantive conversations.

## Measurement

Review qualified inquiries, partner participation, event engagement, follow-up conversations, and content reuse. Favor signals of genuine buying interest over raw reach. Resourcing remains subject to the Q2 close.
