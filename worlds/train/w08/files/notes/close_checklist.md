# June 2026 close — controller's checklist

Prepared by R. Halloway. Complete in sequence; no shortcuts.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 and paying in July. If one exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities; some months all runs pay in-month.
3. Confirm June prepaid amortization against the prepaid schedule and supporting contracts. Check the GL first to avoid a duplicate, then book any missing June amortization as ONE combined journal entry. Apply the convention that a full month is taken in the month each prepaid item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after verifying what is already recorded in the GL.
5. Check every June vendor bill against the source document and verify the expense-account coding. Reclass anything miscoded.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, only after items 1-5 have been booked.
