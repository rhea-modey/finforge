# Handoff notes from the prior accountant

R. Halloway — a few things that will save you some hunting:

- The bank usually posts its own fees and service charges near the end of the statement. We have always booked those during close rather than during the month, so look there before calling the cash reconciliation finished.
- Please do not assume the June vendor-bill coding is right. A couple of expenses may be sitting in the wrong accounts, and the documents in `bills/` are authoritative.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. The one-off items that showed up in June were never planned, and they are the reason some of the variances look unusually large.
- Payroll is the timing wrinkle this month: the second June run pays in early July. Confirm it in the payroll register and handle the close accordingly.
