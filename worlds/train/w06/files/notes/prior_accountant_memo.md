# Notes from your predecessor

B. Okafor — a few things worth knowing before you finish the close:

- The bank's own service fees and charges usually appear near the end of the statement. We have historically picked those up during the close rather than earlier in the month.
- I moved quickly through June, so the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and reclass the ledger where needed.
- The budget was fixed early in the quarter. The one-off June items were never planned, and they are generally the reason the larger budget-to-actual variances look so pronounced.
- The June 16-30 payroll run does not pay until the first days of July this month. The payroll register has the supporting detail.

— Prior staff accountant
