# June 2026 close — controller checklist

Prepared by B. Okafor. Complete in sequence; no shortcuts.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 and paying in July. If such a period exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities; note that in some months every payroll run pays in-month.
3. Confirm June prepaid amortization against the prepaid schedule, supporting contracts, and the GL. If it has not already been posted, book June amortization as ONE combined journal entry—do not duplicate an existing entry. Apply the convention that a full month is taken in the month an item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after first verifying what is already recorded in the GL.
5. Check each June vendor bill against its source document and verify the expense-account coding. Reclass anything miscoded.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after obligations 1-5 have been booked.
