# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Give Copper Kettle Trading a warmer, more recognizable storefront voice while making it easier for customers to discover useful products, bundles, and seasonal edits.

## Priorities

- Refresh core brand language and visual templates across the shop, email, and social channels.
- Build quarterly merchandising stories around practical home, desk, and gifting themes.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter featuring product demonstrations, care tips, or organizing ideas.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary small brand.
- Develop stronger post-purchase messaging for product care, related items, and replenishment reminders.
- Collect customer questions and reviews for use in product pages and campaign creative.

## Channel approach

Email will carry launches, curated edits, and customer education. Social content will emphasize demonstrations, packing-room details, and short staff recommendations. Partner activity should favor shared audiences and useful content over broad promotional reach.

## Measurement

Review engagement, repeat purchasing, bundle uptake, partner-sourced traffic, webinar attendance, and customer feedback each quarter. Use the Q3 findings to simplify or expand the Q4 campaign mix.

## Open items

Confirm campaign owners, creative capacity, webinar topics, partner shortlist, and final resourcing after the Q2 close.
