# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Bluebird Wholesale Co. as the practical regional supplier for customers who value responsive service, sensible assortments, and straightforward fulfillment.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand voice and core sales materials without losing the company’s familiar, approachable tone.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, centered on inventory planning, supply-room efficiency, and seasonal ordering.
- Pilot a partner co-marketing program with selected vendors and complementary service providers.
- Build reusable product spotlights for email, sales follow-up, and account reviews.
- Give the sales team short customer stories and category talking points that are easy to personalize.

## Quarterly emphasis

**Q3:** Complete the messaging refresh, prepare the webinar format, and recruit pilot partners. Test a regular email cadence with existing customers.

**Q4:** Expand the strongest webinar topics, publish partner content, and equip account managers with year-end planning materials.

## Measures

Track registrations, attendance quality, customer replies, sales-team usage, partner participation, and follow-up conversations. Review results after each campaign and keep only the formats that create useful customer engagement.

Budget remains pending completion of the Q2 close.
