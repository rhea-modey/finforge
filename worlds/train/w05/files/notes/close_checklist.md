# June 2026 close checklist

Prepared by A. Whitcomb. Complete these in sequence and initial the workpapers as you go.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 that pays in July. If such a period exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities; note that in some months every payroll run pays in-month.
3. Confirm that June prepaid amortization agrees with the prepaid schedule and supporting contracts. Check the GL first so nothing is recorded twice. If the June entry is missing, book all June prepaid amortization in ONE combined journal entry. Apply the convention that a full month is taken in the month an item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after first verifying the amount already recorded in the GL.
5. Compare the June vendor-bill documents with the GL expense coding. Reclass anything miscoded to the correct expense account.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after all entries and reclassifications from items 1-5 have been booked.
