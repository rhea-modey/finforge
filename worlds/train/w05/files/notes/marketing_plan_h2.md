# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Positioning
Make Beacon Ledger Software the practical close-management choice for lean finance teams. Keep the message grounded in cleaner handoffs, visible ownership, and fewer spreadsheet-driven surprises.

## Q3 priorities
- Refresh the core website narrative and customer proof points.
- Publish a close-readiness guide built around real controller workflows.
- Run recurring educational webinars with product and customer-success participation.
- Recruit a small group of accounting advisers for a partner co-marketing pilot.

## Q4 priorities
- Turn webinar questions into short articles, checklists, and product demonstrations.
- Develop customer stories focused on adoption and day-to-day usability.
- Expand the strongest partner pilot into repeatable joint content.
- Refresh nurture emails for evaluators who are not yet ready for a sales conversation.

## Operating approach
Favor useful, specific material over broad brand claims. Marketing and sales should review lead quality together, while customer success supplies recurring themes from onboarding and support conversations. Final campaign scope remains subject to planning after the Q2 close.
