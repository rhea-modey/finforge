# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Make Ashworth & Bell Advisors easier to understand at a glance: practical advisors who help leadership teams turn plans into operating routines. The tone should stay experienced, specific, and human—not glossy or overly corporate.

## Priorities

- Refresh the core brand language and service-page copy.
- Publish short, useful field notes drawn from recurring client questions.
- Run two webinars per quarter, with one focused on planning and one on execution.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary professional-services firm.
- Build a light referral follow-up rhythm for former clients and trusted introducers.

## Content themes

Focus on decision cadence, management reporting, role clarity, project recovery, and the practical mechanics of turning strategy into accountable work. Each topic should produce a primary article, a shorter email version, and several social posts.

## Delivery notes

Keep production manageable for the consulting team. Subject-matter experts may speak their ideas aloud; marketing can shape the first draft and return it for technical review. Webinars should favor a useful working session over a long presentation.

## Measures

Track qualified conversations, webinar attendance, partner-sourced introductions, repeat site visits, and newsletter replies. Review results quarterly and retire activities that create attention without credible client interest. Budget remains pending the Q2 close.
