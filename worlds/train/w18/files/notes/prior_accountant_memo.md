# A few handoff notes

C. Reyes — a few things I learned the hard way:

- The bank's own service fees and charges usually appear near the end of the statement. They are not typically in the books beforehand, so pick them up during the close.
- I was moving too quickly in June, and the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and reclass the GL where needed.
- The budget was fixed early in the quarter. The one-off June items were never planned, so expect them to explain the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- Payroll timing is unusual this time: the second June run pays in early July. Check the payroll register and handle the accrual accordingly.
