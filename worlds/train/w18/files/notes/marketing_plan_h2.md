# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Keep the brand warm, useful, and unfussy. The creative refresh should sharpen our visual system without making Larkspur Home Goods feel overly polished or distant. Product photography, email, and social copy should emphasize real rooms, practical styling, and items that earn their shelf space.

## Priorities

- Complete the brand refresh and roll the updated look across core customer touchpoints.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter, with topics drawn from customer questions about styling, storage, gifting, and seasonal hosting.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary lifestyle brand.
- Improve product-detail content for priority collections, including dimensions, care guidance, and in-room context.
- Build repeat-purchase email journeys around replenishment, gifting, and coordinated product pairings.

## Channel approach

Email remains the dependable storytelling channel. Social should mix finished-room inspiration with candid packing-table and merchandising content. Paid media will support proven products and selected launches rather than carrying the entire plan. Partner activity should focus on shared audiences and genuinely useful content, not logo swapping.

## Measurement and cadence

Review reach, engagement, conversion quality, repeat ordering, and customer feedback each quarter. The team will document what worked, what felt forced, and what deserves another test. Final channel allocations remain pending the Q2 close.
