# June 2026 close checklist

Prepared by C. Reyes. Complete these steps in sequence; no shortcuts.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 and paying in July. If one exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities; some months, every payroll run pays in-month.
3. Confirm June prepaid amortization against the prepaid schedule, supporting contracts, and the GL. If it has not already been posted, book June prepaid amortization as ONE combined journal entry. Do not duplicate an existing entry. Apply the convention that a full month is taken in the month a prepaid item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after first checking the GL to confirm it has not already been recorded.
5. Verify the expense coding on June vendor bills by comparing the bill documents with the GL. Reclass anything miscoded to the correct expense account.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after all entries and reclassifications from steps 1-5 have been booked.
