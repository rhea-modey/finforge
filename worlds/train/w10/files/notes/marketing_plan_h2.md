# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Give Wildwood Supply Co. a warmer, more recognizable look without losing the practical tone customers already know. The refresh should emphasize useful goods, honest product guidance, and the people who pack and support each order.

## Priorities

- Roll out the brand refresh across the storefront, email templates, packaging inserts, and core social profiles.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, built around organization, everyday carry, and practical care tips rather than hard-sell product demos.
- Pilot a partner co-marketing program with a complementary maker or small business. Start with shared editorial content, then test a coordinated campaign if the audience fit is strong.
- Improve repeat-purchase messaging through post-purchase education, replenishment reminders, and curated follow-up recommendations.
- Build a reusable library of customer photos, short product demonstrations, and behind-the-scenes fulfillment stories.

## Quarter focus

**Q3:** Complete creative direction, refresh priority channels, recruit webinar guests, and select a pilot partner.

**Q4:** Expand the strongest refreshed campaigns, run the partner pilot, and package webinar material into evergreen email and social content.

## Measurement

Track qualified traffic, email engagement, repeat ordering, webinar participation, and partner-sourced visits. Review results by channel and document which messages should carry into the next planning cycle. Budget remains pending the Q2 close.
