# Handoff notes from your chatty predecessor

L. Mbeki — a few things I learned the fussy way:

- The bank slips its own fees and charges in near the end of the statement. We normally book those during close, not as they occur during the month, so do not assume they are already in the ledger.
- Give the June vendor bills a careful coding review. A couple may be sitting in the wrong expense accounts, and the documents in `bills/` are authoritative if the GL description tells a different story.
- The budget was fixed early, at the start of the quarter. The one-off June items were never planned, and they are usually the explanation for the big budget-to-actual variances.
- Payroll timing is a little different this month: the second June run pays in early July. Check the register before booking the accrual.
