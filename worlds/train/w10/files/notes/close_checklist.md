# June 2026 close — controller checklist

Prepared by L. Mbeki. Complete in sequence; no shortcuts.

1. Reconcile operating cash to the June bank statement. For every book-side item identified by the reconciliation, prepare one correcting journal entry per item.
2. Review the payroll register for any pay period ending after 6/30 that pays in July; some months all runs pay in-month. If such a period exists, accrue the full gross wages to Accrued Liabilities.
3. Confirm June prepaid amortization against the prepaid schedule, contracts, and GL. If it is not already posted, book June prepaid amortization as ONE combined journal entry; do not duplicate an existing entry. Take a full month of amortization in the month an item begins.
4. Book June depreciation per the fixed asset register, after verifying the GL to prevent a duplicate posting.
5. Check June vendor-bill documents against the GL, verify expense coding, and reclass anything miscoded.
6. Prepare June budget-vs-actual variance commentary on an as-closed basis, after obligations 1-5 have been booked.
