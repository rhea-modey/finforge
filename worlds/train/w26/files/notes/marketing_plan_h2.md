# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Give Maple & Main Retail Co. a warmer, more recognizable point of view without losing the practical tone customers already trust. The refresh should show up consistently across the storefront, email, packaging inserts, and partner materials.

## Priorities

- Tighten the visual system and simplify core brand messages.
- Build seasonal campaigns around gifting, useful home goods, and workday upgrades.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, using product demonstrations and practical gift-planning themes rather than hard-sell presentations.
- Pilot a co-marketing program with a complementary partner whose audience overlaps with ours.
- Develop reusable photography, short-form video, and customer stories for organic and paid channels.
- Improve post-purchase messaging so first-time buyers have a clear reason to return.

## Quarter focus

### Q3

Complete the brand refresh, update campaign templates, test webinar formats, and identify a partner for the pilot. Establish a simple reporting rhythm for channel performance and content reuse.

### Q4

Apply the refreshed brand to seasonal merchandising, expand the strongest webinar concept, and launch the partner campaign. Coordinate email, social, and storefront storytelling around a shared editorial calendar.

## Open items

Channel mix and final scope remain subject to the Q2 close and team capacity. Owners and production deadlines will be assigned after leadership review.
