# Notes from the previous chair

B. Okafor — a few practical things I learned the long way:

- The bank's own service fees and charges usually appear near the end of the statement. They are historically picked up and booked during close, so do not assume the ledger already has them.
- Please give June vendor-bill coding a real look. A couple of expenses may be sitting in the wrong accounts, and the documents in `bills/` are authoritative if the GL description is vague.
- The budget was fixed early, at the start of the quarter. The one-off June items were never planned, and they are generally the explanation for the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- For payroll timing, trust the register. The pay date for the back-half run varies: in some months it falls in the next month, but in other months every run pays in-month. Do not infer the timing from prior closes.
