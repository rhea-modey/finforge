# Notes from the previous desk

D. Lindqvist — a few things worth knowing before you close the month:

- The bank's own service fees and charges usually show up near the end of the statement. We have historically picked them up during close, so do not stop the reconciliation early.
- Please scrutinize the June vendor-bill coding. A couple of expenses may be sitting in the wrong accounts, and the supporting documents in `bills/` are authoritative if the ledger description disagrees.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. One-off items that arrived in June were therefore never planned, and they are the likely explanation for the larger variances.
- Payroll timing is not automatic here. The pay date for the second monthly run varies, so check the payroll register rather than assuming whether it pays in-month or later.
