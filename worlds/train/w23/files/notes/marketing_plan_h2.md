# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Keystone Freight & Supply as the practical, responsive wholesale partner for teams that cannot afford delays in the shipping room or warehouse.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand voice around reliability, product know-how, and straightforward service.
- Update core sales materials so product categories and delivery capabilities are easier to scan.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, focused on packaging choices, warehouse readiness, and common fulfillment bottlenecks.
- Pilot a co-marketing program with a complementary regional partner.
- Build a repeatable customer-story format for email, web, and sales follow-up.
- Re-engage dormant accounts with useful operational content rather than broad promotional messages.

## Quarter plan

### Q3

Finalize messaging, revise the primary product overview, prepare webinar topics, and select the partner pilot. Establish a simple approval process so sales and marketing use consistent language.

### Q4

Publish customer stories, continue the webinar series, evaluate the partner pilot, and refine outreach based on engagement and sales-team feedback.

## Measures

Track qualified inquiries, webinar attendance, follow-up conversations, reactivated accounts, partner-sourced leads, and sales-team use of the refreshed materials. Budget remains pending Q2 close.
