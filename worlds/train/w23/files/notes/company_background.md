# Company background

Keystone Freight & Supply began in 2008 when two former warehouse supervisors saw that local businesses needed a dependable source for everyday shipping materials without the complexity of dealing with national catalogs. What started in a borrowed corner of a distribution building grew through practical service, accurate orders, and a willingness to track down awkward items for regular customers.

Today, Keystone Freight & Supply is a small wholesale operation serving manufacturers, repair shops, offices, and regional logistics teams. Its catalog centers on cartons, protective packing materials, labels, warehouse consumables, loading supplies, and other goods that keep shipping rooms moving. The company also coordinates delivery and freight handling when customers need more than a standard drop-off.

The team has a distinctly hands-on character. Sales staff know many buyers by voice, warehouse employees flag unusual order patterns before they become problems, and the back office keeps a close eye on vendor paperwork. Decisions are usually made around a shared worktable rather than through layers of meetings.

The current focus is steady execution: maintaining dependable stock, tightening purchasing routines, improving account follow-up, and making the company easier to do business with. Management is also refreshing its market presence while preserving the responsive, practical service that built the customer base.
