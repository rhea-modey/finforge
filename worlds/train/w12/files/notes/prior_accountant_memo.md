- The bank tends to post its own fees and service charges near the end of the statement. That is normal here. We book those at close rather than recording them during the month.
- Please give the June vendor-bill coding a careful look. I was moving quickly, and one or two expenses may have landed in the wrong accounts. Treat the supporting documents in `bills/` as authoritative and reclass the GL where needed.
- The Q2 budget was locked early in April. As a result, the one-off items that arrived in June were never included in the plan; those unplanned items are the main reason the large variances will stand out.
- Payroll timing matters this month: the back half of June pays in early July. The payroll register contains the dates, so use it when preparing the accrual.
