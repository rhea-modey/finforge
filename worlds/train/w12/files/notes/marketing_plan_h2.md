# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Clearpath Partners LLC as the practical partner for teams that need strategy translated into organized, usable action. The tone should remain direct, capable, and human—less consultancy theater, more experienced people getting the work moving.

## Priorities

- Complete the brand refresh, including message hierarchy, service descriptions, and a lighter visual system.
- Publish a steady mix of short field notes, client-friendly guides, and behind-the-scenes perspectives from the delivery team.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter, each built around a recurring operational problem rather than a broad thought-leadership theme.
- Pilot partner co-marketing with a complementary professional-services firm.
- Create reusable follow-up materials so webinar attendees and referrals receive a clear next step.

## Quarter focus

**Q3:** Finish core messaging, refresh priority web pages, prepare the webinar format, and recruit the first co-marketing partner.

**Q4:** Run the partner pilot, continue the webinar cadence, repurpose the strongest material, and review which topics generated useful conversations.

## Measures to watch

Track qualified inquiries, partner-sourced conversations, webinar attendance quality, content engagement, and movement from first conversation to scoped opportunity. Final channel choices remain subject to the Q2 close and leadership review.
