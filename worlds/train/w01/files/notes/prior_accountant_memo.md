# Notes from your predecessor

P. Guerrero — a few things that will save you some hunting:

- The bank tends to place its own fees and charges near the end of the statement. We book those during close, not as they appear during the month, so do not overlook the last section.
- June got busy and some vendor-bill expense coding may be wrong. Use the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative, compare them with the ledger, and reclass where needed.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. The one-off items that arrived in June were never included, and they are the reason several variances look unusually large.
- Payroll is not on the usual in-month pattern this time: the second June run pays in early July. Check the payroll register before booking the accrual.
