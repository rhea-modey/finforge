# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## What we are trying to do

Make Cirrus Metrics Inc. easier to recognize, easier to understand, and easier for operating teams to evaluate. The message should stay practical: connected metrics, less spreadsheet work, and reporting people can actually use.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand system and core website language without turning the project into a wholesale repositioning.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, built around common reporting problems rather than product demos alone.
- Pilot partner co-marketing with a small group of implementation consultants and adjacent software providers.
- Develop customer stories that show the before-and-after workflow in plain language.
- Give sales a tighter set of one-page explainers for integrations, onboarding, and reporting use cases.

## Channel approach

Use email and LinkedIn to distribute practical guides, webinar clips, and customer examples. Keep search content focused on high-intent operational reporting topics. Test partner newsletters and joint events before committing to a broader program.

## Measures of progress

Track qualified conversations, webinar attendance and follow-up, partner-sourced interest, content engagement, and movement from initial evaluation into product review. Report results by quarter and include qualitative feedback from sales and customer success.

## Open items

Final sequencing depends on Q2 learnings, design capacity, and partner readiness. Owners and launch checkpoints still need confirmation.
