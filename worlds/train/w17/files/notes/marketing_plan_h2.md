# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## What we want to accomplish

- Refresh the Quartz Relay Inc. story around reliable workflow communication, not generic messaging.
- Give operations and engineering buyers clearer examples of where the platform fits.
- Build a repeatable partner co-marketing motion without creating a heavy program.
- Turn customer questions into useful educational content for prospects.

## Core programs

### Brand and website
Tighten the homepage narrative, simplify product language, and replace broad claims with concrete use cases. Keep the visual refresh light enough that the team can ship it without distracting from product work.

### Webinars
Run two practical sessions per quarter. Favor live product walkthroughs, troubleshooting clinics, and conversations with operators over formal slide presentations. Reuse each session as short clips, an article, and sales follow-up material.

### Partner pilot
Select a small group of complementary software providers for shared guides, guest sessions, and mutual introductions. Start hands-on; document what works before turning it into a formal playbook.

### Customer proof
Develop concise stories focused on implementation, day-to-day operations, and measurable workflow improvements. Obtain approval early and offer anonymous formats where needed.

## Quarter emphasis

- Q3: messaging refresh, first webinar cycle, partner outreach, and content inventory.
- Q4: publish customer proof, expand the strongest partner activity, and refine webinar topics from audience feedback.

## Open items

Confirm owners, production capacity, channel mix, and success measures after the Q2 close.
