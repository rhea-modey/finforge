# A few handoff notes from your predecessor

P. Guerrero — a few things worth knowing before you settle into the June close:

- The bank's own service fees and charges usually show up near the end of the statement. We have historically picked those up as part of the close, so give the final statement pages a careful look.
- June vendor-bill coding deserves a second pass. Some expenses may have been posted to the wrong accounts; when the GL and the paperwork disagree, the documents in `bills/` are authoritative.
- The budget was fixed early in the quarter. The one-off June items were never planned, and they are generally the reason the larger budget-to-actual variances look so pronounced.
- Payroll timing matters this month: the back half of June pays in early July. The payroll register contains the relevant dates, so use it when determining the accrual.
