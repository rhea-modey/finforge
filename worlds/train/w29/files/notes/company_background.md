# Company background

Bluewater Stack Co. began in 2019 when two former operations leads grew tired of watching small service teams coordinate customer work through sprawling spreadsheets, inbox threads, and improvised status meetings. They built a lightweight internal tool, shared it with a few peers, and discovered that the same problem appeared across agencies, consultancies, and distributed project teams.

Today, Bluewater Stack Co. sells a cloud-based workflow platform that brings client intake, recurring task management, approvals, and operational reporting into one place. The product is deliberately practical: quick to configure, easy for nontechnical users to understand, and flexible enough to fit the way a team already works. Customers also receive implementation guidance and ongoing support from people who know the product firsthand.

The company remains a small SaaS business with a close-knit, slightly informal team. Product and support compare notes constantly, salespeople are expected to understand real customer workflows, and internal chat tends to mix careful release planning with strong opinions about coffee and keyboard shortcuts.

Bluewater Stack Co. is currently balancing steady customer service with routine product improvements and a refreshed marketing effort. The team is tightening operating processes, documenting institutional knowledge, and preparing for the next phase of measured growth while continuing business as usual.
