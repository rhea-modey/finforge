# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Make Bluewater Stack Co. easier to recognize and easier to understand. The story should stay grounded in practical workflow problems rather than broad transformation language.

## Priorities

- Refresh the core brand presentation across the website, sales materials, and customer-facing templates.
- Publish customer-led content showing how small teams replace spreadsheet-heavy operating routines.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, with topics shared across product, support, and sales.
- Pilot a partner co-marketing program with complementary consultants and service providers.
- Build a reusable library of short product demonstrations for campaigns and sales follow-up.

## Audience and message

Focus on operations leaders, client-service managers, and founders who need clearer ownership and fewer manual handoffs. Lead with fast adoption, flexible workflows, and responsive support.

## Working cadence

Campaign themes will be planned by quarter. Marketing will meet regularly with sales and support to review common objections, customer language, and promising stories. Webinar follow-up should be timely and useful rather than overly promotional.

## Measures

Track qualified conversations, webinar engagement, partner-sourced interest, content reuse by sales, and conversion through the evaluation journey. Final channel emphasis remains subject to the Q2 close and team capacity.
