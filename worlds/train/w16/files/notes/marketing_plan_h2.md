# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Summit Ridge Advisors as the practical advisory team that turns good intentions into workable operating routines. Keep the voice direct, experienced, and useful rather than overly polished.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand story and core service descriptions.
- Publish short field notes based on recurring client questions.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter, each built around a concrete operating challenge.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with complementary service firms.
- Create a simple follow-up path for referrals, webinar attendees, and past clients.

## Content themes

- Moving from planning to implementation
- Building management routines that teams will actually use
- Clarifying ownership across growing organizations
- Making reporting more useful for decisions
- Selecting and managing outside vendors

## Channel approach

Use the website as the source of record, with professional-network posts and partner newsletters directing readers back to practical resources. Favor concise case examples, checklists, and workshop clips over broad thought-leadership claims.

## Quarter-by-quarter emphasis

### Q3
Finalize messaging, update priority pages, prepare the webinar format, and recruit pilot partners.

### Q4
Run the partner pilot, continue webinars, repurpose strong sessions into written resources, and review which topics generate qualified conversations.

## Open items

Confirm internal owners, production capacity, review workflow, and final budget after the Q2 close.
