# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction
Position Vantage Loop Inc. as the practical workflow layer for teams that have outgrown shared spreadsheets but do not want a complicated enterprise rollout. Keep the message concrete: clearer ownership, fewer missed handoffs, and faster adoption.

## Priorities
- Refresh the core brand language and visual system without changing the company’s approachable tone.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, built around real operating problems rather than broad product tours.
- Pilot a co-marketing program with selected implementation and advisory partners.
- Publish short customer stories that show how repeatable workflows improve day-to-day coordination.
- Tighten the path from campaign response to product demonstration and follow-up.

## Quarterly sequence
### Q3
Complete messaging work, update high-traffic web pages, prepare the webinar format, and recruit partners for the pilot. Build a reusable campaign kit for sales and customer success.

### Q4
Expand the strongest webinar topics, launch partner-led content, and test industry-specific landing pages. Use customer questions to shape practical guides and demonstration scripts.

## Measurement
Track qualified engagement, webinar attendance and follow-through, partner-sourced conversations, content-assisted demonstrations, and movement through the evaluation process. Review results jointly with sales and customer success.

## Open items
Confirm campaign ownership, creative capacity, partner participation, and final resourcing after the Q2 close.
