# Notes from your predecessor

N. Vartanian — a few things that will save you some hunting:

- The bank tends to post its own fees and service charges near the end of the statement. We have historically booked those during close rather than during the month, so do not assume they are already in the ledger.
- June moved quickly, and the expense coding on vendor bills may be off. Compare the entries to the source documents and treat the files in bills/ as authoritative.
- The budget was fixed early, at the start of Q2. The one-off June items were never included, and they should explain most of the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- Payroll timing matters this month: the back half of June pays in early July. The payroll register contains the applicable dates.
