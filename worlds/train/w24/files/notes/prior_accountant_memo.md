# Notes from your predecessor

D. Lindqvist — a few things worth knowing before you settle into the close:

- The bank tends to slip its own fees and service charges onto the last few lines of the statement. That is normal here; we have historically booked those at close rather than during the month.
- June got busy, and some vendor-bill expense coding may be off. Check the underlying paperwork and treat the documents in `bills/` as the authoritative source. If the GL disagrees, the bill wins.
- The budget was locked early in the quarter. The one-off June items were not contemplated when it was set, so they should account for most of the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- One payroll caution: do not guess based on the usual cadence. The pay date for the second monthly run varies. Check the payroll register and follow the date shown there.
