# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction
Position Stonebridge Professional Services as the practical partner for organizations that need experienced project leadership without unnecessary ceremony. The voice should be confident, useful, and human.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand story and core service pages so prospects can quickly understand what Stonebridge does, how engagements work, and when to call.
- Publish client-friendly insights drawn from recurring operational problems, with an emphasis on checklists, field notes, and short case examples.
- Host two webinars per quarter. Keep the format conversational and leave room for live questions.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with complementary legal, accounting, technology, or creative firms.
- Build a simple follow-up rhythm for former clients, referral partners, and qualified prospects.

## Quarter focus

### Q3
Complete messaging work, update priority web pages, assemble the first webinar topics, and recruit pilot partners. Create reusable promotion and follow-up templates.

### Q4
Run the partner pilot, continue the webinar series, and turn the strongest sessions into articles and short-form posts. Gather client language that can sharpen future case studies.

## Measures
Track qualified conversations, referral activity, webinar attendance, follow-up engagement, and opportunities influenced by content. Review results at each quarter-end and keep only the activities that produce credible interest.
