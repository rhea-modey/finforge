# A few handoff notes

E. Nakamura — a few things I learned the hard way here:

- The bank tends to place its own fees and charges near the end of the statement. We book those during close, not during the month, so do not assume the ledger is finished before checking the last part of the statement.
- I was moving too quickly in June, and the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the supporting documents in `bills/` as authoritative and correct the GL to match them.
- The budget was fixed early in the quarter. The one-off June items were never planned, and they are usually the reason the larger budget variances look so pronounced.
- This month, the June 16-30 payroll run pays in the first days of July. The payroll register has the detail, so make sure the timing is reflected in the close.
