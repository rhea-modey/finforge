# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Give Meridian Basket Inc. a cleaner, more recognizable voice without losing the practical tone customers already know. The refresh should emphasize useful products, candid descriptions, and reliable fulfillment rather than lifestyle hype.

## Priorities

- Refresh core brand language and visual guidelines during Q3.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, built around organization, product care, and small-space routines.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary retailer or service brand.
- Improve product-page storytelling with clearer use cases, packaging details, and customer questions.
- Build reusable email and social content from webinar topics rather than creating isolated campaigns.

## Channel approach

Email remains the primary retention channel. Paid media should support proven product groups and partner activity, while organic social focuses on demonstrations, staff picks, and behind-the-scenes fulfillment content. Avoid broad campaigns that cannot be tied to a defined audience or merchandising goal.

## Measurement

Review webinar attendance quality, partner-referred traffic, repeat engagement, and product-page behavior each quarter. Capture lessons from the Q3 pilot before expanding partner activity in Q4.

Resource allocation remains provisional pending completion of the Q2 close.
