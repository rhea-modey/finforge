# Notes from the previous accountant

E. Nakamura — a few things I learned the hard way:

- The bank tends to place its own fees and charges near the end of the statement. We normally pick those up during close rather than booking them throughout the month, so read all the way down.
- I was moving quickly in June, and the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and adjust the GL to match them.
- The budget was fixed early in Q2. The one-off June items were not planned, and they should explain most of the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- Please do not guess on payroll timing. The pay date for the second monthly run varies; check the payroll register before deciding whether an accrual is needed.
