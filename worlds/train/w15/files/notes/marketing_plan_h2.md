# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Keep the Ironline Distributors Inc. message practical: dependable wholesale supply, responsive people, and fewer headaches for customers managing routine purchasing.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand voice and core sales materials without losing the straightforward, established feel customers recognize.
- Run two useful webinars per quarter, centered on inventory planning, packaging choices, and smoother replenishment workflows.
- Pilot a co-marketing program with selected supplier partners. Favor educational content and customer examples over generic promotion.
- Build a repeatable email rhythm for current customers, lapsed accounts, and qualified prospects.
- Give sales a compact set of product sheets and follow-up templates that can be adapted by industry.
- Capture more customer feedback from routine service conversations and turn recurring themes into content.

## Quarterly emphasis

**Q3:** Complete messaging work, update priority materials, launch the webinar series, and select partners for the pilot.

**Q4:** Expand the strongest campaigns, publish practical customer stories, and review which channels are producing worthwhile conversations.

## Measures

Track webinar attendance, sales follow-up, partner participation, email engagement, repeat inquiries, and qualified opportunities. Review results jointly with sales and operations so campaigns reflect available products and realistic fulfillment capacity.

Budget remains pending Q2 close.
