# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Give Foxglove Market Inc. a warmer, more consistent brand voice across the storefront, email, social channels, and package inserts. The refresh should feel useful and human rather than overly polished.

## Priorities

- Update core messaging and visual guidelines, then apply them first to high-traffic product and collection pages.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, using practical themes such as workspace organization, thoughtful gifting, and care for everyday goods.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary independent brand. Favor shared editorial content and audience fit over broad reach.
- Build campaign briefs around seasonal customer needs, with merchandising, inventory, and customer support represented before launch.
- Repurpose webinar questions into short guides, email features, and social posts.

## Measurement

Track qualified site visits, email engagement, repeat purchasing, webinar participation, partner-referred traffic, and customer-service themes. Review results after each campaign and record what should be repeated, revised, or dropped.

## Open items

Final channel mix and production scope remain pending the Q2 close. Confirm team capacity before committing to creative work, and keep contingency concepts ready for products with uncertain availability.
