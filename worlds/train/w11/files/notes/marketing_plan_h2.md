# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction
Position Cascade Supply Partners as the responsive wholesale partner for teams that value dependable sourcing, practical substitutions, and a person who actually answers.

## Priorities
- Refresh the core brand language without losing the straightforward, local feel.
- Publish customer-friendly guidance on packaging, workplace supply planning, and avoiding fulfillment delays.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, led jointly by sales and purchasing.
- Pilot a partner co-marketing program with selected vendors.
- Give account managers reusable follow-up notes, product spotlights, and customer stories.

## Quarterly emphasis

### Q3
Complete messaging and visual cleanup. Build the webinar format, prepare the first topic series, and identify vendor partners willing to contribute useful expertise rather than sales pitches. Test a simple re-engagement campaign for quiet accounts.

### Q4
Expand the strongest Q3 content into sales tools and short customer emails. Continue the webinar cadence, publish the partner pilot results, and develop seasonal planning materials for customers with recurring supply needs.

## Working principles
Keep the voice practical, warm, and specific. Avoid inflated claims. Marketing should make the sales conversation easier, not louder.

## Measurement
Track attendance quality, account-manager follow-up, customer replies, partner participation, and sales conversations influenced by each activity. Final resourcing remains subject to the Q2 close.
