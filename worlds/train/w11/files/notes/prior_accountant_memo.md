# Notes from your chatty predecessor

N. Vartanian — a few things I learned the hard way:

- The bank slips its own fees and charges in near the end of the statement. Those are booked during close, not as the month rolls along, so do not assume the ledger already has them.
- June moved fast, and some vendor-bill expense coding may be off. Compare the GL with the source documents and treat the files in bills/ as authoritative.
- The budget was locked early in Q2. The one-off June work was never included, which is why it should explain most of the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- This month, the June 16-30 payroll run pays in the first days of July. The payroll register has the support, so make sure the timing is reflected in the close.

That is the short version. Start with the source documents and the reconciliations; they will keep you out of trouble.
