# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Keep Harborline Supply Co. practical and recognizable: dependable stock, responsive people, and straightforward wholesale service. The refresh should feel cleaner, not corporate.

## Priorities

- Update core brand language across sales sheets, email templates, and customer-facing materials.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, centered on inventory planning, workplace supply readiness, and smoother ordering.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary regional vendor.
- Build short customer stories that show how reliable fulfillment helps day-to-day operations.
- Give the sales team reusable follow-up notes and simple campaign talking points.

## Quarter plan

### Q3

Finalize messaging, refresh the most-used materials, select webinar topics, and recruit a pilot partner. Reconnect with dormant accounts using service-led outreach rather than discount-heavy promotion.

### Q4

Expand the strongest webinar theme, publish customer stories, review partner-pilot results, and prepare recommendations for the next planning cycle.

## Measures to watch

Track webinar attendance, qualified follow-up conversations, reactivated accounts, partner-sourced inquiries, and use of refreshed materials by the sales team. Budget remains pending completion of the Q2 close.
