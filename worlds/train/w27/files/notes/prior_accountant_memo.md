# A few handoff notes

L. Mbeki — these are the things I would keep beside the close checklist:

- The bank usually puts its own fees and charges near the end of the statement. We do not book those during the month; pick them up at close.
- I moved quickly through June processing, so the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and correct the GL to match them.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. Any one-off items that arrived in June were not planned, and they are the reason some of the variances look large.
- For payroll, trust the register on the back-half run's pay date. In some months that run slips into the next month; do not assume the timing without checking.
