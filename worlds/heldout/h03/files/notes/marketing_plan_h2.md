# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Keep the Foxhall Consulting Group voice practical, senior-led, and free of management jargon. The aim is to make the firm easier to recognize and easier to refer.

## Priorities

- Refresh core messaging and visual templates so proposals, case studies, and event materials feel like one brand.
- Publish short field notes built around recurring client problems: stalled implementations, unclear ownership, and overloaded operating teams.
- Run two useful, discussion-led webinars per quarter, with follow-up summaries for attendees and prospects.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary services firm. Favor a joint briefing or workshop over a generic promotional campaign.
- Build a lightweight referral kit for former clients and professional contacts.
- Improve consultant bios and service pages with clearer examples of how engagements work.

## Rhythm by quarter

### Q3

Finalize messaging, update the standard content library, launch the first webinar sequence, and identify the pilot partner.

### Q4

Deliver the partner activity, continue the webinar cadence, publish client-safe case examples, and review which channels produced credible conversations.

## Measures

Track qualified introductions, webinar participation, follow-up meetings, partner-sourced opportunities, content reuse, and feedback from active clients. Keep reporting simple enough that the team will maintain it.
