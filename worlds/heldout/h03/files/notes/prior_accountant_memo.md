# Notes from your predecessor

N. Vartanian — a few things worth knowing before you finish the close:

- The bank usually posts its own fees and service charges near the end of the statement. We have historically booked those at close rather than during the month, so check the final statement activity carefully.
- Please do not trust the first-pass coding on every June vendor bill. A couple may be sitting in the wrong expense account. The documents in `bills/` are authoritative; use them as the source of truth and reclass the GL where needed.
- The Q2 budget was locked in early April. One-off items that arrived in June were never included, and they are the main reason some variances will look unusually large.
- Payroll timing is not fixed. Do not assume the second monthly run pays in July: its pay date varies, so verify the actual timing in the payroll register.
