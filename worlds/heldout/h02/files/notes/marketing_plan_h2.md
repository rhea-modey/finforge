# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction
Give Saltbox Mercantile Co. a warmer, more consistent visual voice while keeping the practical, unfussy tone customers recognize.

## Priorities
- Roll out the brand refresh across the storefront, email templates, packaging inserts, and social profiles.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter, centered on gifting, workspace organization, and buying from independent makers.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with a complementary maker or workplace-services brand.
- Build seasonal editorial stories that connect products by use rather than by category alone.
- Improve post-purchase messaging with care tips, maker stories, and sensible cross-sell recommendations.

## Quarter focus
**Q3:** Finish creative standards, update core channels, recruit pilot partners, and establish webinar production routines.

**Q4:** Launch seasonal campaigns, expand the strongest partner concept, and reuse webinar material across email and social content.

## Measures to watch
Track qualified traffic, email engagement, repeat purchasing, webinar attendance, partner-sourced visits, and conversion by campaign theme. Keep reporting concise and separate durable gains from short promotional spikes.

Budget remains pending completion of the Q2 close.
