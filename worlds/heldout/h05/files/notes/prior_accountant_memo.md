# Notes from the old desk

B. Okafor — a few things that will save you some hunting:

- The bank tends to add its own fees and service charges near the end of the statement. Those usually are not in the books yet; our practice has been to pick them up during close.
- June moved fast, and I would not trust every vendor bill to have landed in the right expense account. Use the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and correct the coding where needed.
- The budget was locked early in Q2. The one-off June work came later and was never planned, so those items should explain most of the larger budget-to-actual variances.
- One payroll timing item matters this month: the June 16-30 payroll run does not pay until the first days of July. The payroll register has the support.

— Prior staff accountant
