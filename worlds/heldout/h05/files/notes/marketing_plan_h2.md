# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## What we want to accomplish

- Refresh the Lakeside Family Health LLC brand without losing the warm, neighborhood-clinic feel.
- Improve awareness of primary care, preventive visits, and workplace health services.
- Build a repeatable referral path with employers and community organizations.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter.

## Priority audiences

- Families and adults looking for an accessible primary-care relationship
- Local employers seeking practical workplace health support
- Community partners whose clients need a trusted clinic referral

## Planned work

**Brand refresh:** Tighten the visual system, simplify service descriptions, and update core website and handout language.

**Educational content:** Develop plain-language webinar topics around preventive care, workplace wellness, and preparing for routine visits. Reuse each session as short articles and social posts.

**Partner pilot:** Test a co-marketing program with a small group of aligned local organizations. Provide ready-to-share copy, referral guidance, and a simple contact route.

**Patient communications:** Use friendly reminders and service spotlights, with an emphasis on usefulness rather than sales language.

## Measurement

Track inquiries, appointment requests, webinar attendance, partner referrals, and engagement with refreshed service pages. Review results at the end of each quarter and adjust the channel mix. Final scope remains subject to the Q2 close.
