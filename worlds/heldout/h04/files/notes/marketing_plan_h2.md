# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Anvil Works Manufacturing as the responsive, plain-spoken choice for specialized fabrication and short production runs. The message should emphasize practical engineering support, reliable communication, and parts built to customer specifications.

## Priorities

- Refresh the brand language and visual system without losing the shop-built character customers recognize.
- Publish concise capability stories showing how the team handles unusual drawings, tight handoffs, and repeatable production.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter for operations, sourcing, and engineering contacts.
- Pilot co-marketing with a complementary regional supplier.
- Improve follow-up materials for inquiries, quote requests, and dormant accounts.

## Content themes

Focus on manufacturability, material selection, inspection practices, short-run planning, and avoiding delays between design approval and production. Keep the tone useful rather than promotional; shop photos and operator commentary should do more work than generic claims.

## Channels

Use the company site as the primary destination, supported by email, professional social posts, webinar invitations, and partner referrals. Sales should receive short, reusable follow-up notes tied to each campaign theme.

## Quarter rhythm

In Q3, complete the messaging refresh, build the first capability stories, and launch the webinar cadence. In Q4, expand the strongest topics, test the partner pilot, and review lead quality with sales.

## Open items

Confirm internal owners, production availability for photography, and final spending after the Q2 close.
