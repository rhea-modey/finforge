# A few handoff notes

B. Okafor — a few things I learned the hard way:

- The bank slips its own fees and charges in near the end of the statement. We normally pick those up during close, not as they occur during the month, so do not overlook the last section.
- I was moving too fast in June, and the expense coding on one or two vendor bills may be wrong. Treat the source documents in `bills/` as authoritative and correct the GL to match them.
- The budget was locked early in Q2. None of the one-off June projects made it into the plan, and those items should account for most of the large budget variances.
- Payroll timing is the other catch: the back half of June pays in early July. The payroll register contains the actual dates, so use it for the accrual support.
