# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Ember Signal Inc. should sound like the calm operator in a noisy category: practical, credible, and close to the work. The H2 program will tighten the brand story around clearer signals, faster ownership, and less operational clutter.

## Priorities

- Refresh core messaging and visual guidance without forcing a wholesale rebrand.
- Publish customer-led material that shows real monitoring and incident workflows.
- Run two educational webinars per quarter, alternating between operator roundtables and product walkthroughs.
- Test a partner co-marketing pilot with complementary SaaS providers and specialist consultancies.
- Give sales a smaller, cleaner set of proof points, email sequences, and follow-up assets.

## Quarter plan

### Q3

Finalize positioning, update high-traffic pages, interview customers, and prepare the first webinar series. Select partner candidates based on audience fit and willingness to share execution.

### Q4

Launch the co-marketing pilot, expand the strongest webinar themes into written content, and review lead quality with sales. Retire channels that create activity without useful conversations.

## Measurement

Track qualified engagement, webinar attendance quality, partner-sourced conversations, content-assisted opportunities, and sales feedback. The team will review results after each campaign and document what to repeat, revise, or stop.
