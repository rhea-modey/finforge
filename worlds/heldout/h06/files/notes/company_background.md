# Company background

Foundry Line Industries began in 1998, when a toolmaker and a production supervisor set up a modest job shop to serve regional equipment builders that needed dependable short-run parts without a long procurement cycle. The business grew through repeat work, practical engineering support, and a reputation for calling customers early when a drawing or tolerance looked likely to cause trouble on the floor.

Today, Foundry Line Industries manufactures machined brackets, housings, guards, fittings, and other made-to-order metal components. Its customers span industrial equipment, marine, laboratory, and specialty-product markets. Work typically moves from drawing review and material planning through machining, fabrication, finishing, inspection, and shipment.

The team has the familiar character of a small manufacturer: experienced operators who know the machines by sound, newer technicians cross-training across cells, a purchasing lead who keeps close tabs on lead times, and office staff who handle customer schedules with a direct, no-fuss style. Production and accounting work closely because job timing, material receipts, and vendor paperwork often cross month-end.

The company is currently focused on keeping delivery performance steady, tightening documentation, and broadening relationships with repeat customers. Management is also refreshing its market presence while preserving the responsive service and shop-floor judgment that built the business.
