# H2 marketing plan — working draft

## Direction

Position Foundry Line Industries as the practical manufacturing partner for buyers who value responsive quoting, clear production communication, and dependable short-run component work.

## Priorities

- Refresh core messaging around machining, fabrication, finishing, inspection, and schedule visibility.
- Build customer-facing examples that show how the team resolves drawing, tolerance, and manufacturability questions before they disrupt production.
- Host two educational webinars per quarter for engineers and purchasing teams.
- Pilot partner co-marketing with a complementary finishing, materials, or equipment business.
- Improve follow-up with past customers and dormant accounts using concise, industry-specific outreach.

## Content themes

- Practical design-for-manufacture guidance
- Shop-floor quality checks
- Material and lead-time planning
- Short-run and repeat-order coordination
- Employee craft and cross-training

## Quarter focus

### Q3

Finalize the brand refresh, update service-page copy, collect shop photography, and prepare the first webinar series. Select a co-marketing partner and agree on shared topics and review responsibilities.

### Q4

Publish customer-use stories, continue the webinar cadence, evaluate partner-pilot engagement, and refine outreach by market segment. Capture sales-team feedback on lead quality and recurring customer questions.

## Measures

Track qualified inquiries, webinar attendance, follow-up conversations, returning-account engagement, and sales feedback. Budget remains pending completion of the Q2 close.
