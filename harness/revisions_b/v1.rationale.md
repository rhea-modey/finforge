## REFLECTION

**(a) Top recurring failure modes**

1. **Variance analysis: wrong-period totals.** Critiques 9 and 11 both call this out: *"The submission is built on the wrong period and therefore almost every number is wrong… the task requires JUNE-only budget-vs-actual… The submission instead used Q2/YTD cumulative actuals."* Critique 4 reinforces: *"Product Sales actual is 53,678… NOT 70,965. The submission used the June AR debit total."* The Analyst is defaulting to the trial balance (cumulative) and to AR debits (non-revenue) instead of filtering the GL detail to the requested period and using revenue-account credits.

2. **Fabricated reconciling items in bank rec.** Critique 5: *"The claimed 'gl_error' of 8,317.00 'unrecorded cash receipt' is fabricated — no such amount or unrecorded receipt exists."* Critique 12: *"The proposed 8,102.28 'cash overstatement' entry to Retained Earnings is a fabricated plug."* Critique 12 also flags *"six old checks… (total 18,432). These are April/May payments"* wrongly listed as outstanding. The Reviewer has only `read_file`, so it cannot recompute balances or cross-foot, and fabricated items slip through.

3. **Missed vendor-bill reclasses.** Critiques 2, 3, and 10 all flag the same omission — a bill coded to an obviously wrong account in the GL is never reclassed: *"checklist item 5 (vendor-bill reclass) was not performed."* The Analyst never reads `bills/` to compare GL coding against the bill's stated nature.

4. **Cited non-existent or prior-period bills as evidence.** Critique 9: *"cites BILL-3133… and BILL-3135 (an April Bright Harbor bill) as advertising evidence."* Critique 11: *"BILL-3130… and BILL-3131… are April bills, not June."* No mechanism requires each cited filename to exist or to belong to the current period.

**(b) Mapping to spec components**

- Failure 1 → **Analyst TOOLS** (no `run_python`, so cannot filter the GL by month or recompute) and **Analyst INSTRUCTIONS / CONTRACT** (no rule against using cumulative balances for a single period).
- Failure 2 → **Reviewer TOOLS** (no `run_python`, cannot recompute adjusted balances) and **Reviewer CONTRACT** (no requirement to flag fabricated items).
- Failure 3 → **Analyst INSTRUCTIONS** (no explicit step to cross-check GL coding against bills in `bills/`).
- Failure 4 → **Analyst CONTRACT** (no per-figure evidence trail) and **WORKFLOW** (no revision pass after Reviewer flags concerns).

**(c) Changes chosen and which failure each one answers**

1. **Give the Analyst `run_python` and require period-filtering from the GL detail, revenue-account credits (not AR debits), evidence per figure, bill-reclass checks against `bills/`, and forbid invented reconciling items.** *(answers failures 1, 3, 4)*
2. **Give the Reviewer `run_python` + `grep_files` and require it to independently recompute every figure and verify task-specific properties (period correctness, balance tie, JE balance, reclass legitimacy).** *(answers failure 2)*
3. **Add a one-shot revision loop in the workflow** so Reviewer concerns can be routed back to the Analyst before submit, and tighten the Orchestrator's instructions to pass filenames and the chart of accounts path explicitly so the Reviewer doesn't waste calls searching (as it did in trace 2). *(answers failures 1, 2, 4 by giving the system a recovery path)*

Sections that served categories at or above their historical best (accrual_schedule at 7.67) are preserved — the FileScout→Analyst→Reviewer flow and the basic Analyst/Reviewer role shape are intact; only the failures above are tightened.
