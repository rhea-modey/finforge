Looking at the v4 evidence, I see several recurring failure modes that map clearly to spec components. Let me identify the top ones and the targeted changes I'll make.

**Top failure modes:**

1. **Reviewer is a rubber stamp** — multiple critiques explicitly call this out ("The Reviewer made a single 8-character call, which is a rubber stamp rather than a real review"; "review was a rubber stamp (Reviewer made a single 302-token call)"). The Reviewer contract lets the agent return "APPROVED" without verifying per-field schema presence or checklist coverage. *→ Maps to ROLES/INSTRUCTIONS/CONTRACT of Reviewer.*

2. **Completeness gaps — checklist items dropped without explicit declaration** — five+ critiques complain that depreciation, reclasses, accruals, or amortization were silently omitted ("the task explicitly required... yet the workpaper books only X... and simply asserts 'no depreciation or reclass entries were needed' without showing the fixed_asset_register or other accrual sources were e[ven]"). The Analyst contract requires no item-by-item mapping. *→ Maps to Analyst CONTRACT (missing coverage map) and Analyst INSTRUCTIONS.*

3. **Variance drivers are reverse-engineered, not decomposed** — three critiques highlight this ("every 'driver' is a generic restatement of the full variance ('Freight expense variance', 'Revenue variance') rather than a specific initiative/vendor decomposition, so the drivers explain nothing and simply duplicate the variance figure"; "every driver amount is set exactly equal to the full account variance"). The current rule forbids generic descriptions but not the plug-equality pattern. *→ Maps to Analyst INSTRUCTIONS / AUXILIARY RULES.*

4. **Bank-reconciliation plugs and inverted side labels** — "the bank side... and the book side... do not agree, and the stated adjusted balance... is only reached by mixing sides and then forcing a '$46.98 Miscellaneous Expense' plug... side labels are also inverted". v4 forbids plugging in the bank rec paragraph but the Calculator doesn't independently enforce it, and there's no rule forcing the Reviewer to re-check side labels. *→ Maps to Calculator INSTRUCTIONS / AUXILIARY RULES.*

5. **journal_entry_status asserted without ledger evidence** — three critiques note "the trace shows the Analyst grepped the ledger... which suggests the amortization may already be recorded in the GL, yet the workpaper asserts 'to_book'". The status field is required but its evidentiary basis isn't. *→ Maps to Analyst/Calculator INSTRUCTIONS.*

6. **Budget-period basis mismatch undetected** — "the budget file is a quarterly (Q2) figure being compared against a single month's actual; if so, every variance is overstated". No rule forces the Analyst to verify the budget file's period basis before differencing. *→ Maps to Analyst INSTRUCTIONS.*

**Changes I'm making (1–3 targeted):**

- **Change A — make the Reviewer walk schema_blueprint and the checklist.** Replace the free-text "APPROVED or list of concerns" contract with a per-field schema walk, a per-checklist-item coverage walk, and a per-driver-evidence walk. Categorize each concern by type so it can't be waved off as a single yes/no. (Answers failure modes 1, 2, 3.)
- **Change B — require an Analyst coverage map and forbid plug drivers.** The Analyst must enumerate every schema_blueprint field AND every checklist item as `in_draft` (with draft path + evidence) or `not_applicable` (with scope/file justification). Variance drivers must each derive from a specific source document amount, and any residual must be reported as an explicit `unexplained` driver — never absorbed into a generic one. (Answers failure modes 2, 3.)
- **Change C — Calculator + Reviewer enforce no-plug, side-label, status-evidence, and budget-basis rules.** Add explicit Calculator checks for these and Reviewer concern categories. (Answers failure modes 4, 5, 6.)

These three changes share a common thread: they convert soft conventions in v4 ("must", "should", "do not") into structured contract fields the downstream agents must enumerate, so a Reviewer can't rubber-stamp them.
