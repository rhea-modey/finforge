## Reflection

**Failure modes identified:**

1. **Bank rec fails to tie out, with a placeholder gl_error line shipped anyway** — "bank side 399,519.78 - 26,654.38 = 372,865.40 while book side 410,886.22 - 65.90 + 13.08 = 410,833.40, and the stated adjusted balance of 426,121.34 matches neither side" (trace 1, w19-bank_rec). Maps to **CONTRACTS** (Calculator must not return APPROVED on a non-tying rec) and **ORCHESTRATOR INSTRUCTIONS** (the "submit anyway with notes" loophole lets broken recs ship).

2. **Completeness claims with no visible evidence** — "the note that 'payroll accrual was $0' is asserted without any supporting computation" (critique 3, journal_entries); "the workpaper never demonstrates that the May 31 prepaid file ... was fully enumerated" (critique 6, accrual). Maps to **ANALYST INSTRUCTIONS** — the completeness_map needs per-item evidence rows, not free-text status strings.

3. **As-closed basis not applied to variance actuals** — "Depreciation Expense actual 0.00 vs budget 2,717.68 ... Insurance Expense 0.00 vs 157, even though the checklist close entries (depreciation, prepaid amortization) were supposed to be booked first" (critique 11, variance). Maps to **ANALYST INSTRUCTIONS** — the as-closed basis rule is stated but not enforced as a pre-compute gate.

4. **Generic, circular, or self-contradictory drivers** — "'Unexpected freight surge', 'Increased advertising spend' ... rather than the specific initiatives/vendors the task demanded"; "the 4000 Wholesale Sales driver is self-contradictory -- it describes 'revenue higher than budget' while the line is a $3,582.51 unfavorable shortfall" (critique 8). Maps to **ANALYST INSTRUCTIONS** + **CALCULATOR CONTRACT** — Calculator must reject drivers without a vendor/invoice/document reference and check sign-language matches variance sign.

**Changes chosen (each ≤1 targeted edit):**

- **Change A** — Add a `severity: "blocking" | "non_blocking"` field to Calculator's issue objects, define blocking as tie-out/balance/schema failure, and rewrite the orchestrator to refuse to submit when any blocking issue remains after the revision loop (closing the bank-rec plug-and-ship loophole). Answers failures 1 and indirectly 4.

- **Change B** — Rewrite the Analyst's completeness_map requirement so every status row is a structured object with `{file, line_or_date, evidence_kind, expression, conclusion}` rather than a free string; require a run_python proof of the bank-rec tie-out (or an explicit "unbalanced" flag) before the Analyst may call its draft complete. Answers failures 1 and 2.

- **Change C** — Insert an explicit as-closed-basis pre-compute gate in the variance branch of the Analyst: list the checklist close entries, post them in working_notes, then re-derive each actual from `GL + posted close entries`, and have the Calculator re-derive actuals the same way. Answers failure 3.

- **Change D (light touch)** — Tighten the driver requirement: every driver must cite a specific vendor/invoice/initiative/document filename, and must carry the sign of its variance; Calculator rejects drivers with no document reference or wrong sign as blocking. Answers failure 4.

RATIONALE: Iteration v7 is based on v6 and targets four recurring failure modes from the v6 evidence. The dominant fix is the new blocking/non_blocking severity field on Calculator issues plus an explicit "blocking issues gate" in the orchestrator, which closes the loophole that let a non-tying bank rec with a zero-amount gl_error placeholder ship to submission (failure 1). The Analyst's completeness_map is rewritten as a structured row array with mandatory file/line/date/computation fields, which forces the visible per-item evidence that v6 lacked for payroll accrual, prepaid enumeration, and depreciation (failure 2). A new "as-closed basis gate" forces the Analyst to post checklist close entries to working actuals before computing variances, and requires the Calculator to recompute the same way, which closes the v6 regression where Depreciation/Insurance actuals were reported as zero against non-zero budgets (failure 3). Driver discipline is tightened to require a vendor/invoice/document reference and a sign matching the variance, with the Calculator instructed to flag missing-reference or sign-mismatched drivers as blocking, which addresses the v6 generic-driver and self-contradictory-driver critiques (failure 4). Reviewer max_steps is reduced and capped at one post-Calculator call to prevent runaway reviewer loops seen in the v6 traces.
