## REFLECTION

Looking at the evidence, I see three dominant failure modes that are highly consistent across nearly every sample:

**Failure 1: No arithmetic verification, ever.** Across all 12 critique groups the judge calls out that "no run_python (or any computation) call appears anywhere in the trace, so the [amounts] are asserted rather than verified." Truncated traces show zero `run_python` calls and instead long sequences of `read_file`/`grep_files`. This maps to **CONTRACTS** (the Analyst's contract doesn't require verified, reproducible arithmetic) and **WORKFLOW** (no dedicated verification hop). → *Answered by adding a Calculator agent that independently recomputes everything with `run_python`.*

**Failure 2: Conceptual / completeness errors the LLM gets wrong on its own.** Quoted fragments: *"outstanding checks and the deposit in transit are bank-side timing differences, not book-side items"*; *"the drivers sum to the actual rather than to the variance"*; *"the task explicitly asks for a roll-forward 'for each prepaid item'"*; *"Salaries Expense actual of 37,935 reflects only the 2026-06-01 to 06-15 half-month payroll"* (missing close entries before computing as-closed actuals). These map to **INSTRUCTIONS / CONTRACTS** — the Analyst is never told the conventions (which side is which, drivers tie to variance not actual, apply standard close entries first, enumerate ALL items). → *Answered by sharpening the Analyst's contract with explicit definitions, completeness requirements, and a mandatory working_notes block.*

**Failure 3: The Reviewer is broken.** Traces show the Reviewer spends ~20-27 calls probing for nonexistent files (`draft_answer.json`, `output.json`, `manifest.txt`, `README.md`, `close_data.json`) returning 20-60 char stubs, then "approves" anyway. The review step provides zero signal. Quoted: *"the Reviewer's empty-path read_file calls errored out, so the 'reviewer approved' note reflects a hollow check."* This maps to **WORKFLOW / AUXILIARY RULES** — there is no gate requiring substantive review. → *Answered by giving the Reviewer a narrower, tractable role (schema + spot-check after Calculator has done the substantive verification) and adding an acceptance gate.*

RATIONALE: v1 is based on v0 and makes three targeted changes addressing the three dominant failure modes in the evidence. (1) **Added a Calculator agent with run_python** that independently recomputes every tie-out, schedule roll-forward, driver sum, and journal-entry balance, and rejects drafts whose figures cannot be reproduced within $0.01 -- this fixes the universal "no arithmetic verification" failure. (2) **Sharpened the Analyst's contract and instructions** to require (a) explicit enumeration of every checklist/prepaid/reconciling/variance item with a booked/needs_entry/not_applicable status, (b) correct bank-rec side-labeling conventions (bank-side = outstanding checks, DIT, bank errors; book-side = fees, interest, unposted items) and a two-column tie-out, (c) variance drivers that sum to the variance (not the actual), and (d) a working_notes block recording every run_python expression so verification is reproducible -- this fixes the conceptual and completeness errors (inverted sides, drivers summing to actual, dropped prepaids, missing as-closed entries). (3) **Rewrote the Reviewer** to do a narrow, tractable check (schema + spot-check that the Calculator's corrections were applied and cited files exist) and added an **acceptance-gate auxiliary rule** that the Calculator's APPROVED verdict is required before submission, fixing the broken rubber-stamp review. FileScout's role is preserved but its manifest is now categorized with full paths.
