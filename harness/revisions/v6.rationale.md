## REFLECTION

Looking at the v5 evidence, the dominant failure modes are clear and map cleanly to specific spec components:

**Failure 1 — Process thrash and unbounded loops.** "CUR is a textbook case of process thrash: 376 tool calls, 246 Analyst LLM calls, 60 Reviewer LLM calls" and "CUR burned 186 Reviewer LLM calls and duplicated FileScout reads." Maps to **WORKFLOW** (revision loop cap too loose, agents re-called too freely) and **ORCHESTRATOR** (no hard submission checkpoint).

**Failure 2 — Plug/fictional reconciling items and driver residuals.** "the team simply booked that exact residual as a 'gl_error' described as 'Adjust GL cash balance to reconcile with bank statement' — a circular plug" and "the majority of nearly every variance is dumped into a placeholder line literally labeled 'unexplained variance' whose only 'evidence' is the trial balance." Maps to **INSTRUCTIONS for Analyst** (no explicit anti-plug rule) and **AUXILIARY RULES**.

**Failure 3 — Incomplete deliverables / checklist items silently dropped.** "the deliverable addresses only one of the roughly five in-scope checklist obligations" and "the prepaid amortization is booked as insurance only ($85) despite the instruction to combine all prepaid amortization into one entry." Maps to **CONTRACT for Analyst** (no completeness map required) and **WORKFLOW** (no enforcement that every checklist item gets a verdict).

**Failure 4 — Runs that never submit.** "SUBMITTED ANSWER: none (run did not submit)" — twice on variance tasks. Maps to **ORCHESTRATOR instructions** (no mandatory submit checkpoint) and **AUXILIARY RULES** (acceptance gate permits aborting instead of submitting a draft).

I base v6 on **v4** (incumbent, mean 5.88) and make three targeted tightenings:

1. **Cap revision loop at ONE pass** (down from two) and require the orchestrator to always call `submit_answer` with whatever draft it ends with.
2. **Re-introduce explicit anti-plug rules** (forbid "gl_error", "balancing", "reconciliation adjustment", and "unexplained variance" residual drivers) and require every figure to trace to a specific source document with date and ref.
3. **Require the Analyst contract to include a `completeness_map`** naming every checklist item as delivered / already_booked_with_evidence / out_of_scope_per_schema, with the GL line date inside the close period for any "already_booked" claim.

RATIONALE: v6 branches from v4 (incumbent, mean 5.88) and addresses the four failure modes that drove v5's regression: (1) **process thrash** — capped revision loop at one pass, lowered orchestrator max_steps to 40, added an explicit "FileScout called once" rule; (2) **plug/gl_error items and "unexplained variance" drivers** — re-introduced a strong anti-plug rule across Analyst, Calculator, Reviewer, and AUXILIARY RULES, and required residuals be reported as `residual_unexplained` in working_notes rather than booked as items; (3) **incomplete deliverables** — required the Analyst contract to include a `completeness_map` classifying every checklist item with evidence; (4) **runs that never submit** — added a hard rule that the orchestrator MUST always end with `submit_answer` and a non-empty notes string. All other v4 structure (FileScout/Analyst/Calculator/Reviewer roles, schema_blueprint discipline, evidence integrity checks, sign conventions, amortization rules, date conventions) is preserved.
