## REFLECTION

**Failure mode 1 — Generic variance drivers that don't sum to the variance.** Across multiple variance_analysis samples, drivers were placeholder strings ("Revenue variance driver", "General ledger postings") with amounts set equal to the full account actual rather than the variance; sample 11 actually had revenue actuals negative vs positive budgets ("sign-convention mismatch that makes the variance arithmetic meaningless"). *Quoted fragment: "the two revenue lines carry negative actuals (-432,507 and -23,828) against positive budgets, which is a sign-convention mismatch."* This maps to the **Analyst INSTRUCTIONS + CONTRACT** for variance work — neither currently forces revenue-vs-expense sign normalization nor a per-account "named driver → sums to variance" check.

**Failure mode 2 — Checklist items silently dropped in journal_entries and accrual_schedule.** Both categories showed books-only-payroll / one-prepaid-line deliverables even though the source clearly had more items (BILL-3115/3116, software_license, multiple prepaid rows). *Quoted fragment: "the deliverable looks materially incomplete: the task asked for accruals, amortization, depreciation, and any needed reclasses, yet only a payroll accrual, a single $123 insurance amortization, and depreciation were booked."* This maps to **AUXILIARY RULES + Analyst CONTRACT** — the "do not silently drop items" rule exists but has no enforcement in the Calculator/Reviewer step (no count match between checklist and draft).

**Failure mode 3 — Bank-rec `side` labels inverted.** Multiple bank_rec samples had outstanding checks / deposits in transit tagged "book" and bank fees / interest tagged "bank", even though the underlying arithmetic ties out. *Quoted fragment: "bank fees and interest are tagged 'bank' and outstanding checks/deposits in transit are tagged 'book', which is inverted from standard bank-rec convention."* This maps to **Analyst INSTRUCTIONS + Reviewer CONTRACT** — the convention is mentioned but never verified.

**Failure mode 4 — Wasted FileScout reads with `max_lines: 1–5`.** The traces show dozens of one- or five-line reads that can't actually ground any figure. This maps to **FileScout INSTRUCTIONS**.

**Changes chosen and what each answers:**
1. **Tighten the Analyst variance instructions and contract** (sign-normalization step, named drivers that sum to the variance, evidence-citation requirement, explicit per-account footing check in working_notes) → answers FM1.
2. **Promote "every checklist item has status + evidence + count match" from advisory to enforced** in the Analyst contract, Calculator checks, and Reviewer contract → answers FM2.
3. **Make the bank-rec `side` convention both explicit in instructions and an explicit Reviewer/Reviewer-reject condition**, and have FileScout skip irrelevant bills/invoices when the task isn't a variance task → answers FM3 + FM4.

RATIONALE: Based on v1. Three targeted changes to address the dominant failure modes shown in this iteration's evidence: (1) the Analyst's variance instructions and contract now require an explicit sign-normalization step for revenue vs expense accounts and force every driver to name a specific vendor/initiative/invoice/payroll event with a source citation, with the Calculator required to verify these; (2) the "every checklist item gets a status and evidence" rule is promoted from advisory text to a hard contract field, an explicit Calculator check, and a Reviewer count-match requirement, addressing the journal_entries/accrual completeness gaps; (3) the bank-rec `side` convention is stated more explicitly and made an automatic Reviewer reject condition, addressing the consistent side-label inversion across bank_rec samples. FileScout instructions also tightened to skip irrelevant bills/invoices and avoid one-line reads, addressing the wasted-call pattern visible in the traces. Structure preserved: same four agents, same workflow order, same acceptance gate. Role fields are now kept on a single line to satisfy the validator's field-order check.
