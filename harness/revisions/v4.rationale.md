## REFLECTION

Analyzing the v3 evidence (mean 5.00 vs incumbent v1's 5.83), four failure modes recur:

**1. Schema non-compliance** — The Analyst routinely invents or renames the deliverable's JSON fields. Critique: *"it uses 'gl_cash_balance' instead of 'gl_cash_ending_balance'... uses 'bank_side_items'/'book_side_items' instead of the required 'reconciling_items' array with kind/amount/date/ref/description/side."* This maps directly to **CONTRACTS** — neither FileScout's contract, the Analyst's contract, nor the Reviewer's contract forces field-name conformance to the task's stated schema.

**2. Fabricated or unverifiable evidence** — Critique: *"the reviewer confirms the cited bank statement line numbers (19, 23, 27) do not exist... evidence references point to files that do not exist... all '42559' greps returned header-only."* This maps to **INSTRUCTIONS and AUXILIARY RULES** — agents are not constrained to cite only paths that appear in the manifest, and there is no rule requiring every cited source to actually exist in the surveyed files.

**3. Scope drift driving cost and incompleteness** — The accrual_schedule trace shows *"91 Analyst calls, ~754k input tokens, 141 tool calls largely on out-of-scope close-checklist items (bank rec, payroll, depr...)."* Critique: *"Checklist item 6 (budget-vs-actual commentary) is entirely absent while items 1 and 5 are marked not_applicable without any concrete GL/source citation."* This maps to **INSTRUCTIONS** — the Analyst is told to enumerate the entire task checklist even when the deliverable schema only requires a subset.

**4. Sign and convention errors** — Critique: *"the favorable-variance driver amounts should be negative but were left positive... 'Higher material costs' is directionally backwards for a favorable variance"* and *"amortizing a 12-month license in full in the month it begins."* This maps to **INSTRUCTIONS** (conventions) and **CONTRACTS** (Calculator checks). Dates in non-ISO format (*"reconciling-item dates are '08-Jun-2026' style"*) reinforce the same gap.

**Chosen changes (3, targeted):**
- **(a) Extract `schema_blueprint` from the task description in FileScout's contract and pipe it through every hop.** This forces the Analyst and Reviewer to conform to a concrete, quoted schema blueprint rather than improvising field names. (Addresses failure 1.)
- **(b) Add an explicit scope-discipline rule and a manifest-evidence rule.** The Analyst must produce only what the schema blueprint asks for, and every cited file path must appear in the manifest; the Calculator and Reviewer verify both. (Addresses failures 2 and 3.)
- **(c) Tighten variance/amortization conventions and add an explicit sign-convention check in the Calculator.** Favorable expense variances must be reported as negative driver amounts; multi-month prepaids must amortize over the stated term. (Addresses failure 4.)

RATIONALE: Based on v1 (incumbent, best mean 5.83) which kept the working 4-agent FileScout→Analyst→Calculator→Reviewer structure. v2 and v3 added checklist enumeration and stricter conventions but drifted into scope bloat and schema improvisation that drove scores down to 5.17 and 5.00. v4 preserves v1's lean structure but adds three targeted fixes: (1) FileScout extracts an explicit `schema_blueprint` from the task description and pipes it through every hop so the Analyst must conform to verbatim field names and the Reviewer can enforce this — addressing the dominant "wrong field names" failure; (2) scope-discipline and manifest-evidence rules stop out-of-scope checklist exploration (the 91-call accrual case) and stop fabricated line/file citations, enforced as Calculator checks and Reviewer concerns; (3) tightened variance and amortization conventions (favorable = negative, multi-month over term, period-end dates, deposit-in-transit requiring an actually-missing bank entry) plus explicit enum/date-format checks in the Calculator and Reviewer cover the recurring sign, amortization, and date-format bugs.
